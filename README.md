A make.fun entry for **One Word From Grandma**: exactly one word — the word grandma would text about AI.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the word to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Al

Grandma reads "AI" and sees a man's name — the same warm, baffled misreading as "LOL" meaning "lots of love". The joke is the abbreviation itself: two letters, tied to AI and nothing else, in the voice of a grandmother who thinks the robot has a name.
